Below is the safest order. **Do not factory reset until Step 1 is fully done.**

## 1) Lockout-proof your accounts first

### A. Google account — most important

1. On a **laptop/PC or another phone**, open a private/incognito browser and sign in to your Google account.
    
2. Confirm you know the **exact Google password**.
    
3. Go to **Google Account > Security**.
    
4. Under recovery options, confirm your **recovery phone** and **recovery email** work. If you add/change recovery info now, Google says recovery changes can take up to **7 days** to fully help with account recovery, so do not rely only on newly added recovery info. ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/183723?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en&utm_source=chatgpt.com "Set up recovery options - Android - Google Account Help"))
    
5. Go to **2-Step Verification > Backup codes**.
    
6. Generate a **new set of backup codes**.
    
7. Save them **outside the S24 Ultra**: print them, write them on paper, or store them in a password manager/account that is not locked behind the same Google account. Google says backup codes are specifically for cases where you lose your phone or cannot use Google Authenticator. ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1187538?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en "Sign in with backup codes - Computer - Google Account Help"))
    
8. Test one backup code from the laptop: sign in again, tap **Try another way > Enter one of your 8-digit backup codes**. After testing, mark that code as used because Google backup codes are one-time use. ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1187538?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en&utm_source=chatgpt.com "Sign in with backup codes - Android"))
    
9. After the test, generate a **fresh new set** again so you have 10 unused codes.
    

### B. Google Authenticator

1. Open **Google Authenticator** on the S24 Ultra.
    
2. Tap your profile icon at the top.
    
3. Make sure you are signed in to the correct Google account and **not** using “Use without an account.”
    
4. Google says Authenticator codes sync across devices when you sign in to your Google account, but also says you should add extra 2-Step Verification methods so you do not lock yourself out. ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1066447?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en "Get verification codes with Google Authenticator - Android - Google Account Help"))
    

Important: **Google Authenticator sync is not enough by itself** if your Google login asks for an Authenticator code before you can open Authenticator again. That is why the printed/downloaded Google backup codes are mandatory.

### C. Samsung account

1. On a laptop/PC, sign in to your Samsung account.
    
2. Confirm the Samsung password works.
    
3. Go to **Security / Password and security / Two-step verification**.
    
4. Confirm your phone number is correct.
    
5. Generate or download **Samsung backup codes** and save them outside the phone. Samsung says backup codes can be used when you cannot use your phone for two-step verification. ([Samsung pl](https://www.samsung.com/ae/support/apps-services/accessing-backup-codes-for-your-samsung-account/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Accessing backup codes for your Samsung account"))
    

### D. Every account inside Google Authenticator

Open Google Authenticator and make a list of every code shown: banks, social media, work accounts, crypto, emails, etc.

For each important account:

1. Sign in on a laptop while the S24 still works.
    
2. Go to that account’s **Security / 2FA** page.
    
3. Download or regenerate backup codes.
    
4. Add a second method where possible: SMS, email recovery, security key, or passkey on another device.
    
5. Do not store these backup codes only in Photos, WhatsApp, Google Drive, or the phone itself.
    

## 2) Back up only what you want

### Contacts

1. On the S24 Ultra: **Settings > Google > All services > Backup & restore > Google Contacts sync > Also sync device and SIM contacts**.
    
2. Turn on **Sync device and SIM contacts automatically**.
    
3. Choose the Google account you will use after reset. Google says device/SIM contacts saved as Google contacts sync back when you sign in on a new/reset device. ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/contacts/answer/9423168?hl=en "Back up & sync device & SIM contacts - Contacts Help"))
    
4. Extra safety: open the **Contacts** app > **Organise/Manage contacts > Export to file** and save the `.vcf` file somewhere outside the phone. Google also provides restore steps through **Settings > Google > Set up & restore > Restore contacts**. ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/contacts/answer/7199294?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en "Export, back up, or restore contacts - Android - Contacts Help"))
    

### Photos/videos

1. Open **Google Photos**.
    
2. Tap your profile photo > **Photos settings > Backup**.
    
3. Turn **Backup** on.
    
4. Confirm the **backup account** is the correct Google account.
    
5. Tap **Back up device folders** and enable any folders you care about, especially:
    
    - Camera/DCIM
        
    - WhatsApp Images
        
    - WhatsApp Video
        
    - Downloads, Screenshots, or other folders you need
        
6. Leave the phone on Wi-Fi and charging until Google Photos says **Backup complete**. Google says this is the status shown when upload is finished. ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/photos/answer/6193313?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en "Back up photos & videos - Android - Google Photos Help"))
    
7. On a laptop, open Google Photos and spot-check recent and old photos/videos before resetting.
    

### WhatsApp

1. Make sure the **same SIM/eSIM phone number** will be usable after reset. WhatsApp restore requires the same phone number and Google account used for the backup. ([WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1144861179456352?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Back Up or Restore Your Chats"))
    
2. Open **WhatsApp > ⋮ > Settings > Chats > Chat backup**.
    
3. Select the correct **Google account**.
    
4. Turn **Include videos** on if you want WhatsApp videos restored.
    
5. Tap **Back up** and wait until it finishes.
    
6. If you use WhatsApp encrypted backup, confirm you know the password, 64-digit key, or passkey. WhatsApp says you cannot restore an encrypted backup without the password/key/passkey. ([WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/490592613091019?utm_source=chatgpt.com "About end-to-end encrypted backup"))
    
7. Go to **WhatsApp > Settings > Account > Two-step verification**. Make sure you know the PIN and add an email address, because WhatsApp can email a reset link if you forget the PIN. ([WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1278661612895630?utm_source=chatgpt.com "About two-step verification"))
    

## 3) Factory reset safely

Use the reset from Android/Samsung Settings, **not Recovery Mode / hard reset**. Samsung says Google Device Protection can require the last Google account after a reset from Recovery, and Samsung also says removing/disconnecting Google accounts prevents FRP lock after reset. ([Samsung pl](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10003349/ "Google Device Protection on your Galaxy phone or tablet"))

Recommended path:

1. Confirm you have:
    
    - Google password
        
    - Google backup codes printed/saved outside the phone
        
    - Samsung password
        
    - Samsung backup codes
        
    - WhatsApp PIN and encrypted-backup password/key if enabled
        
    - Same SIM/eSIM number available
        
2. If using **eSIM**, during reset choose the option that **keeps eSIMs** if shown. You need mobile/SMS access after reset.
    
3. Go to **Settings > General management > Reset > Factory data reset**.
    
4. Review the screen.
    
5. Tap **Reset**.
    
6. Enter your phone PIN/password.
    
7. Tap **Delete all**.
    
8. Enter your Samsung account password if asked. Samsung’s official reset steps say factory reset erases apps, settings, personal files, and may ask for lock screen credentials and Samsung account password. ([Samsung pl](https://www.samsung.com/ca/support/mobile-devices/reset-your-samsung-galaxy/ "How to reset your Samsung Galaxy with 5 different methods | Samsung CA"))
    

## 4) Restore only contacts, WhatsApp, photos/videos

During setup:

1. Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data.
    
2. Sign in to your Google account.
    
3. Use your backup code, SMS, security key, or other non-phone method if asked for 2FA.
    
4. When Android/Samsung asks to copy or restore apps/settings, choose **Don’t copy**, **Skip**, or deselect apps/settings. You do not want a full device restore.
    
5. Finish setup.
    

Then restore only what you need:

1. **Contacts:** open Contacts. They should sync automatically. If not, go to **Settings > Google > Set up & restore > Restore contacts**.
    
2. **Photos/videos:** install/open Google Photos, sign in to the same Google account, and confirm your library appears. Photos may be cloud-viewable rather than fully downloaded locally unless you manually download or copy them back.
    
3. **WhatsApp:** install WhatsApp from Play Store, open it, verify the **same phone number**, and when it finds the Google backup, tap **Restore**. Do not skip the restore prompt.
    
4. **Google Authenticator:** install Google Authenticator, sign in to the same Google account, and confirm your codes reappear. Google says Authenticator codes sync when signed in to the Google account. ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1066447?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en "Get verification codes with Google Authenticator - Android - Google Account Help"))
    

My strongest recommendation: before pressing factory reset, do a real login test on a laptop where you can access Google and Samsung using backup codes, not the S24. That single test is what prevents the lockout scenario you had last time.
